# fix_simulation_leaves_natural: a natural (worldgen) oak next to a /fill
# block of oak leaves. Seed 1 has an oak with its trunk at x=-2, z=-2,
# y=70..74 and no other log within 8 blocks. At t=2.5 the trunk is removed:
# the natural canopy must crumble within a few seconds (wake cascade), while
# the 3x2x3 /fill leaves east of it (player-placed, hence persistent) must
# survive the cascade reaching them.
#
# Run:  mabuild scenario tests/scenarios/fix_simulation_leaves_natural.txt --tag fix_simulation
world fix_simulation_leaves_natural 1 creative

0.3  cmd /time set noon
0.3  cmd /weather clear
0.3  cmd /fly
0.5  cmd /fill 2 73 -3 4 74 -1 oak_leaves
1.0  pos -0.5 90 10
1.0  look 0 -55
1.5  hidehud on
2.0  shot fleavesnat_0_before.bmp
2.5  cmd /fill -2 70 -2 -2 74 -2 air
5.0  shot fleavesnat_1_t5.bmp
10.0 shot fleavesnat_2_t10.bmp
30.0 shot fleavesnat_3_t30.bmp
